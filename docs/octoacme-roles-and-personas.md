# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic direction for the product and ensure the team stays aligned to the highest-value outcomes. They work across business, product, and engineering stakeholders to resolve trade-offs and maintain clarity on priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define and refine product direction, roadmap priorities, and business outcomes
- Make prioritization decisions when requirements conflict or resources are constrained
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers to align delivery plans with strategic goals
- Review major scope changes, trade-offs, and release readiness decisions
- Represent product strategy in stakeholder and leadership conversations

### Goals
- Maintain a clear product vision and roadmap
- Align business goals with delivery execution
- Reduce ambiguity in prioritization and decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Weekly product and delivery alignment meetings
- Stakeholder updates and executive briefings
- Prioritization reviews with PMs, engineering leads, and project teams

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to translate customer and business needs into roadmap decisions
- Collaborates with Project Managers to keep planning and milestones aligned to strategic priorities
- Partners with Developers to clarify scope, trade-offs, and technical feasibility
- Escalates unresolved prioritization conflicts to sponsors and senior stakeholders

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define quality standards, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure releases are ready for production with appropriate confidence and risk coverage.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and acceptance validation approach
- Review user stories and requirements for testability and completeness
- Coordinate unit, integration, and end-to-end test planning
- Validate release readiness and track defects or quality risks
- Partner with Developers and Product Leads on quality trade-offs and release decisions

### Goals
- Deliver high-confidence releases with reduced production defects
- Ensure product quality matches customer expectations and acceptance criteria
- Support continuous improvement of testing and verification practices

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and review meetings
- Release readiness checks and defect triage
- Quality updates with PMs, Developers, and stakeholders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to ensure tests cover requirements and regressions
- Collaborates with Product Managers and Product Leads to validate that acceptance criteria are measurable and complete
- Coordinates with Project Managers to align QA timelines with release plans and milestones
- Escalates systemic quality risks or unresolved defects to project leadership

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, funding, and strategic direction. They sponsor the project, confirm priorities, and help resolve escalations when business decisions are needed.

### Responsibilities
- Approve charter, budget, and high-level milestones
- Confirm project alignment with business goals and expected outcomes
- Review progress against success metrics and business value
- Provide decisions on scope, timeline, or priority trade-offs
- Support issue escalation where cross-functional decisions are required

### Goals
- Protect business value and investment in the initiative
- Maintain alignment between project execution and strategic objectives
- Reduce business risk and uncertainty for key project decisions

### Typical Communication
- Milestone and status reviews
- Sponsor meetings and leadership updates
- Escalation decisions for major blockers or changes in scope

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides direction to Product Leads and Product Managers on strategic priorities
- Partners with Project Managers to confirm milestones, staffing, and communication cadence
- Receives regular status updates from PMs and uses decisions to unblock cross-team dependencies
- Works with Security and QA leads when business risk or release approval decisions need executive input

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure that project work meets security requirements, reduces risk exposure, and responds effectively to incidents or vulnerabilities.

### Responsibilities
- Define and validate security requirements for features, services, and releases
- Review architecture, code, and deployment patterns for security risks
- Coordinate security testing, scanning, and risk assessments
- Lead incident triage and response coordination for security-related issues
- Support release approval with security considerations and mitigations

### Goals
- Protect customer data, systems, and organizational trust
- Reduce security-related delays, incidents, and compliance gaps
- Embed security thinking early in planning and delivery

### Typical Communication
- Design and architecture reviews
- Security risk updates and incident response coordination
- Release approval checkpoints and remediation planning

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to review secure design and implementation choices
- Collaborates with Project Managers on risk mitigation and release timelines
- Partners with Product Leads and Stakeholders to assess business impact of security concerns
- Supports QA / Testing Leads by validating security checks are included in quality gates

---

## Agile Coach / Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Agile Coaches or Scrum Masters help the team work effectively, improve delivery practices, and remove friction in the daily workflow.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agile ceremonies such as standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- Help the team improve flow, reduce blockers, and maintain healthy collaboration
- Support the use of project boards, backlog hygiene, and definition of done
- Identify process bottlenecks and propose practical improvements
- Coach individuals and teams on continuous learning and feedback practices

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and delivery consistency
- Create a safe, high-performing environment for feedback and learning
- Help the team continuously improve without adding unnecessary process overhead

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective follow-ups and process improvement tracking
- Coaching sessions with individuals, teams, and leaders

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Project Managers in setting up effective delivery rhythms and governance
- Helps Product Managers and Product Leads refine backlog quality and prioritization discussions
- Works with Developers to improve collaboration, planning quality, and team accountability
- Partners with QA / Testing Leads to improve quality practices and release confidence without slowing delivery

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify accountability, improve cross-functional alignment, and make project decisions more transparent across delivery, product, and stakeholder groups.
