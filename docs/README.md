## OctoAcme Process Summary

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative project lifecycle with clear ownership and evidence-based decisions. Work begins with initiation to confirm the problem, stakeholders, success measures, timeline, risks, and resource needs. Approved ideas move into planning, where the team establishes priorities, estimates work, defines acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, and identifies dependencies and milestones.

During execution, the team works in small increments using the project board, pull requests, automated quality checks, testing, progress reviews, and structured escalation for blockers. Risks and stakeholder communications are managed continuously through a shared risk register and regular updates. Releases require completed acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, staging validation, release notes, and post-deployment verification. After each sprint, release, milestone, or incident, the team captures learnings in a retrospective and tracks a small set of measurable improvement actions.
